													PROJECT REPORT – 5
									Healthcare Management System Using Agile Methodology
									
									
1. Introduction

The Healthcare Management System is a software application designed to manage healthcare-related activities such as patient records, 
appointments, medical history, and hospital administration. The system aims to improve efficiency, reduce paperwork, and enhance the quality 
of patient care.

This project is developed using the Agile Methodology, which supports iterative development, continuous feedback, and adaptability to changing 
healthcare requirements.


2. Problem Statement

Many healthcare institutions still rely on manual or semi-digital processes to manage patient information, appointments, and billing. This 
often results in data inaccuracies, delayed services, and inefficient workflows. A centralized healthcare management system is required to 
streamline hospital operations.


3. Objectives of the Project

To maintain digital patient records

To manage appointments efficiently

To improve coordination between patients and healthcare providers

To reduce administrative workload

To enhance healthcare service quality


4. Scope of the Project

The system supports patient registration, appointment scheduling, doctor management, medical record maintenance, and billing processes. It is 
intended for use by hospitals, clinics, and healthcare centers.


5. Agile Methodology

Agile methodology emphasizes incremental development through short development cycles known as sprints. Continuous feedback from healthcare 
staff ensures that the system meets real-world requirements.

Agile Practices Used

Iterative development

Continuous testing

Frequent user feedback

Incremental feature delivery
